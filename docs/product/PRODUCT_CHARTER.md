# Hut4Devs Product Charter

## Product vision
Build open-source community coordination infrastructure that makes shared responsibilities, peer support, contributions, and trust more transparent, verifiable, and collaborative.

## Product purpose
Hut4Devs is not merely a payment application. It helps communities coordinate real responsibilities and cooperation while preserving consent, privacy, accountability, and human agency.

## Product problem
Communities often coordinate accommodation, contributions, informal support, shared expenses, loans, repayments, and changing responsibilities through fragmented chats, spreadsheets, and personal records. This can create confusion, missing evidence, disputes, and avoidable administrative work.

This problem statement is a working hypothesis. Research must validate the specific users, needs, and highest-value initial problem before implementation expands.

## Intended users
- Community members and residents
- Community coordinators and administrators
- Contributors and supporters
- People giving or receiving peer support
- Open-source contributors and maintainers

Validate each role and its permissions through research.

## Product capabilities over time
1. Community membership, roles, and access boundaries.
2. Accommodation records and shared responsibilities.
3. Contributions, gifts, and peer-support records.
4. Lending, repayment schedules, partial repayments, and renegotiation.
5. Verifiable activity history and trust trails.
6. Notifications, summaries, and workflow automation.
7. Optional AI-assisted capabilities where validated needs justify them.
8. Appropriate blockchain anchoring and integrations.
9. Operational security, resilience, governance, and ecosystem integrations.

These are candidate capabilities, not a commitment to build all of them in the first release.

## Product principles
- Functionality and validated user needs before implementation detail.
- Privacy and consent by design.
- Least-privilege access and explicit authorization.
- Transparent records without exposing unnecessary personal data.
- Human control over consequential decisions.
- Evidence before claims of success.
- Security and accessibility throughout development.
- Small, testable, reviewable increments.
- Open-source collaboration with clear contribution standards.
- No unnecessary blockchain or AI complexity.

## Non-goals
- Replacing every community communication tool.
- Making blockchain mandatory for ordinary application operations.
- Publishing personal, identity, accommodation, or payment details on-chain.
- Using AI as an unquestioned authority for disputes, lending, trustworthiness, or access.
- Adding features solely to follow trends.
- Treating prototype functionality as production-ready.

## Initial product hypothesis
A small community needs a reliable way to record shared responsibilities and contributions, see their status, and resolve changes with a clear history.

Validate this hypothesis before committing to a broad MVP.

## Success measures
Define baselines and targets during discovery. Candidate measures:
- Percentage of users completing the core workflow successfully.
- Reduction in missing or disputed records.
- Time required to reconcile responsibilities.
- User understanding of consent, visibility, and permissions.
- Reliability and recovery performance.
- Security findings resolved before release.
- Contributor onboarding and successful task completion.

## Product authority
The organization-level product documents define direction and boundaries. Repository READMEs define implementation scope. Accepted architecture decisions govern technical trade-offs. GitHub issues track approved work.

# Hut4Devs Product Log

## Purpose
The Product Log records product learning: observations, assumptions, research, experiments, outcomes, and changes in understanding. It is distinct from the issue tracker and the Architecture Decision Log.

## Evidence rules
- Separate direct observations from interpretation.
- Label unvalidated claims as assumptions.
- Record negative and inconclusive results.
- Use consent-appropriate evidence and avoid unnecessary personal data.
- Do not treat a prototype demonstration as proof of product-market fit.
- Record why an experiment was run and what changed afterward.
- Link to source material when permitted and available.

## Initial hypotheses to investigate
1. Communities lose time and trust because responsibilities and contributions are fragmented across tools.
2. A shared, permission-aware record can reduce confusion and disputes.
3. Verifiable trust trails may help in specific cases where independent evidence is valuable.
4. AI assistance may reduce administrative effort without replacing human judgment.

These hypotheses are not established facts. Research must validate, revise, or reject them.

## Product learning cadence
Review new evidence during discovery and at each milestone gate. When evidence changes scope or priorities, update the roadmap and create a decision-log entry where the change is significant.

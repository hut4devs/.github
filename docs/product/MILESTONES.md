# Hut4Devs Milestones and Exit Criteria

## How a milestone is approved
Each milestone must state:
- Problem and intended user outcome.
- Included capabilities and explicit exclusions.
- Dependencies and accountable owner.
- Acceptance criteria and validation method.
- Security, privacy, and operational risks.
- Evidence required for completion.
- Follow-up work and unresolved decisions.

## Completion states
- Proposed: idea exists, not approved for delivery.
- Discovery: gathering evidence and validating the problem.
- Ready: scope, dependencies, and acceptance criteria are clear.
- In progress: implementation or research is underway.
- In review: evidence and changes are being reviewed.
- Accepted: exit criteria have been met and recorded.
- Blocked: a named dependency or decision prevents progress.
- Deferred: intentionally postponed with a reason.

## Gate 0  Governance ready
Required evidence:
- Approved charter, roadmap, boundaries, and issue policy.
- Repository inventory and ownership.
- Product and decision logs.
- Baseline security and contribution standards.

## Gate 1  Problem validated
Required evidence:
- Research notes and consent-appropriate evidence.
- Defined user journey and primary pain point.
- Prioritized requirements and non-goals.
- Baseline and success measures.
- Recorded decision to proceed, revise, or stop.

## Gate 2  MVP usable
Required evidence:
- End-to-end demonstration of the agreed core journey.
- Acceptance tests and negative-path tests.
- Access-control and data-boundary review.
- Deployment, backup, recovery, and support instructions.
- User feedback and known limitations.

## Gate 3  Peer support reliable
Required evidence:
- State-transition rules and invariants.
- Repayment and partial-payment tests.
- Correction, renegotiation, and dispute handling.
- Auditability and permission tests.
- Clear user-facing statuses and limitations.

## Gate 4  Trust trail justified
Required evidence:
- Written on-chain/off-chain decision.
- Threat model and contract interfaces.
- Deterministic contract tests and integration scenarios.
- Testnet verification and resource measurements.
- Reconciliation and failure recovery.
- Explicit production approval criteria.

## Gate 5  AI justified and evaluated
Required evidence:
- Validated use case and baseline without AI where appropriate.
- Defined quality, latency, and cost measures.
- Privacy, authorization, and abuse review.
- Evaluation and regression tests.
- Human fallback and correction mechanism.
- Documented limits and failure modes.

## Gate 6  Security maturity demonstrated
Required evidence:
- Current threat model and tracked risk owners.
- Automated scanning and security test results.
- Incident-response and recovery exercises.
- Vulnerability triage and disclosure procedure.
- No unresolved critical findings at release.

## Gate 7  Sustainable operation
Required evidence:
- Measured service performance and reliability.
- Operational ownership and support procedures.
- Contributor and maintainer feedback.
- Governance and release process.
- Sustainability risks and improvement backlog.

A milestone is not accepted solely because all its GitHub issues are closed. The outcome and evidence must also be reviewed.
